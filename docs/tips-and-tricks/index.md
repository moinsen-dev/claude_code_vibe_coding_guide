## Contributing Tips

Have a valuable tip or technique to share? We welcome contributions from the community:

1. **Test thoroughly** - Ensure your tip works reliably
2. **Provide context** - Explain when and why to use the technique
3. **Include examples** - Show practical implementations
4. **Credit sources** - Acknowledge original creators or inspiration
